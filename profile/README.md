# CivilFox UPSC 🦊

**Read Today's News. We Handle the Rest.**

CivilFox is an EdTech platform designed to make UPSC Civil Services Examination preparation smarter, more structured, and accessible. It transforms important current affairs into exam-oriented learning resources, helping aspirants connect real-world events with the UPSC syllabus.

### 🎯 What We Do

CivilFox bridges the gap between daily news and UPSC preparation by providing:

- **Prelims Preparation:** Exam-oriented MCQs, including statement-based and assertion-reason questions.
- **Mains Preparation:** Structured analytical content and answer-writing resources.
- **Current Affairs:** UPSC-relevant news curated to reduce information overload.
- **Smart Revision:** Spaced-repetition-based learning to improve retention.
- **Integrated Learning:** Connect current affairs with static subjects and important concepts.

### 🚀 Our Mission

To simplify UPSC preparation through technology, intelligent learning tools, and exam-focused content — empowering aspirants to study smarter, revise effectively, and prepare with confidence.
